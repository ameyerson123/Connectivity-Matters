Service 1 Squarespace: Text + Accordion or expandable service block
Service Area 01
Municipal Broadband Strategy & Network Development

Many cities recognize the need for better broadband — but don't know where to start. We guide municipalities through the full arc of network planning: from community needs assessment and ISP landscape analysis, to infrastructure design, public-private partnership structuring, and launch.

Drawing on direct experience building and scaling Newark Fiber — a publicly-run fiber network serving thousands of active Wi-Fi users daily and connected to nearly 100 major business locations — we understand what it takes to move from vision to operational network.

Relevant experience: Architect of Newark Fiber's growth strategy, connecting large businesses, enabling smart-city applications, and sustaining 7,200+ daily active Wi-Fi users. Former Deputy CTO for Broadband for the City of New York, where Aaron led the city's Internet Master Plan — one of the most ambitious municipal broadband efforts in U.S. history.

Feasibility Studies Network Planning Public-Private Partnerships ISP Negotiations Smart City Applications Fiber Infrastructure
Service 2 Squarespace: Alternate-color row for visual rhythm
Service Area 02
Digital Inclusion Program Design & Implementation

Access without adoption is an incomplete solution. We design holistic digital inclusion programs that address all three pillars of the digital divide: affordable connectivity, device access, and digital skills training — with a focus on the communities most likely to be left behind.

Our work includes designing device refurbishment pipelines (including youth workforce programs), establishing community Wi-Fi hubs, coordinating subsidy enrollment campaigns, and building data collection systems to measure program impact and guide continuous improvement.

Relevant experience: Led Newark's digital inclusion efforts that earned National Digital Inclusion Alliance "Trailblazer" recognition three consecutive years (2023, 2024, 2025). Designed the Hope Village 2 connectivity partnership, recognized with the NJ Gov Tech "Best IT Collaboration" award. Structured the Citizens Bank/Newark Fiber partnership delivering 15 years of free internet to South Ward residents.

Program Design Device Access Digital Skills Training Affordable Connectivity Programs Workforce Development Community Partnerships
Service 3 Squarespace: Text block with left gold border accent
Service Area 03
Broadband Policy, Planning & Interagency Coordination

Broadband deployment is rarely stopped by a lack of technology — it's stopped by policy complexity. Permitting bottlenecks, siloed agencies, unclear franchise agreements, and the absence of data all slow communities down. We help untangle these barriers.

Our policy work includes developing broadband development survey programs for real estate permitting processes, designing interagency coordination frameworks, producing community connectivity assessments, and advising on smart building connectivity standards that ensure new development supports — not undermines — long-term digital equity goals.

Relevant experience: As NYC Deputy CTO for Broadband, Aaron architected a single-point-of-contact system across 18 city agencies to streamline vendor access to city real estate — a model now cited nationally. In Newark, launched a Broadband Development Survey embedded in the permitting process to drive developer accountability for connectivity standards.

Broadband Master Plans Permitting Policy Interagency Frameworks Smart Building Standards Connectivity Assessments Digital Equity Planning

Service Area 04
Federal & State Funding Strategy (BEAD, NTIA & Beyond)

The federal broadband funding landscape — BEAD, NTIA programs, FCC initiatives, and state-level grants — represents a once-in-a-generation investment opportunity. But accessing those funds requires more than filling out an application. It requires a strong program narrative, a clear deployment strategy, and compliance infrastructure built from day one.

We help municipalities and nonprofits identify the right funding opportunities, build compelling applications, and design program structures that satisfy grant requirements while serving community needs — and we bring experience from both the public and nonprofit side of these funding relationships.

BEAD Program NTIA Grants Application Development Program Design Compliance Frameworks State Grant Navigation

Service Area 05
Innovation Ecosystem Development & Tech Sector Engagement

Broadband is a foundation — but what communities build on top of it matters just as much. We support municipalities and economic development organizations in leveraging connectivity as a platform for innovation economy growth: attracting high-growth businesses, supporting tech startups, building workforce pipelines, and designing programs that connect residents to the jobs of the future.

This includes facilitating accelerator programs, structuring corporate-community partnerships, and developing smart city strategies that use connectivity to address quality-of-life challenges like urban heat, air quality, and public safety.

Relevant experience: As Chief Innovation Economy Officer at Invest Newark and Newark Alliance, Aaron helped Newark emerge as a nationally recognized innovation hub — covered by Forbes, ROI-NJ, and featured at Newark Demo Day 2024 and 2025. Moderated the NJ FAST program spotlight at VC Day 2025, convening Prudential, Plug and Play, and leading NJ startups.

Full Bio Squarespace: Text block with pullquote styling
Aaron Meyerson — Founder & Principal Consultant

Aaron Meyerson has spent more than a decade helping cities and communities navigate the complex landscape of broadband access, digital inclusion, and connectivity policy. His work spans two of America's most complex urban environments — New York City and Newark, New Jersey — giving him a rare combination of large-scale policy architecture experience and ground-level implementation knowledge.

As Deputy Chief Technology Officer for Broadband for the City of New York, Aaron led the development of New York City's Internet Master Plan — one of the most comprehensive municipal broadband strategies in U.S. history. He built the interagency coordination framework that united 18 city agencies behind a single broadband vision, streamlining access to city-owned assets for ISPs and creating a model that cities across the country continue to study and adapt.

As Chief Innovation Economy Officer and Director of Broadband at Invest Newark and the Newark Alliance, Aaron drove the expansion of Newark Fiber — a municipally-operated broadband network serving thousands of active users and dozens of large commercial customers daily. Under his leadership, Newark was named a National Digital Inclusion Alliance "Digital Inclusion Trailblazer" for three consecutive years, structured multi-million-dollar public-private partnerships to deliver free internet to low-income residents, and won the New Jersey Excellence in Technology "Best IT Collaboration" award.

"By supporting digital inclusion initiatives, we are giving residents support as they build their ladder into the innovation economy and beyond. We're also focused on ensuring the city's booming real estate growth is well connected and enables residents and businesses to thrive." — Aaron Meyerson, NJBIZ, 2026

Aaron is a nationally recognized voice on municipal broadband and digital equity, appearing on the Community Broadband Bits Podcast (Episodes 525 and 587), speaking at NJPAC, Newark Tech Week, NJ Gov Tech, and VC Day, and featured in Forbes, ROI-NJ, NJBIZ, Telecompetitor, and the Benton Foundation. He has served as Faculty for the American Association of Colleges & Universities Institute on Digital Equity.

Aaron founded Connectivity Matters Consulting to bring this depth of experience directly to municipalities, nonprofits, and community organizations across the country that are ready to stop waiting — and start building.

Our Approach Squarespace: 3-column icon-text block · Teal accent
Our Approach
Practical. Equitable. Community-First.

Implementation Experience

We've sat on both sides of the table — as city officials making decisions, and as program architects doing the work. Our advice is grounded in what actually happens when policy meets implementation.

Equity as a Design Principle

Digital inclusion isn't a checkbox — it's the goal. Every recommendation we make is evaluated through the lens of who benefits, who is left out, and how to close that gap.

Cross-Sector Partnership

The best broadband programs don't come from government or the private sector alone. We specialize in designing partnerships that align the incentives of cities, nonprofits, ISPs, developers, and funders.

Honest, Rigorous Analysis

We ask hard questions — about cost efficiency, program reach, and long-term sustainability — so our clients invest in solutions that actually work, not programs that look good on paper.
